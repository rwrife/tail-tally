## Description
A little care, every day.

Was breakfast served? Who took the last walk? Is it time to scoop the litter? Tail Tally helps you keep track of the everyday routines that make your pets feel at home.

Made for iPhone, Tail Tally brings your pets, routines, and care history together in a simple, private organizer. No account required.

A CLEAR PLAN FOR TODAY
See what’s overdue, due now, coming up, and already done. Filter your timeline by pet and mark a routine complete with a tap. Add a note when there’s something worth remembering, or undo a recent completion if you tapped by mistake.

EVERY PET, EVERY ROUTINE
Create profiles for your pets and set weekly schedule windows for feeding, walks, litter care, grooming, and more. Add household members and assign routines so your local care log shows who handled what.

A HISTORY YOU CAN CHECK
Browse today’s activity, the past week, or your full history. See completion times, household attribution, and notes. Correct a note or remove an accidental entry when needed.

REMINDERS ON YOUR TERMS
Choose optional on-device notifications, set how early you’d like a reminder, and add quiet hours. Your timeline and history work even when notifications are off. Open Tail Tally regularly to refresh upcoming reminders.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Tail Tally has no accounts, ads, analytics, or cloud sync. Household data stays on your iPhone. Export a JSON backup, restore a saved household, or save a date-range CSV of your care history. Choose how long to retain history and delete your local data whenever you want.

Designed for one iPhone’s local household log. Household members and assignments do not sync between devices.

Tail Tally is a routine organizer. It does not provide veterinary advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency monitoring.
